Program 01 · Illustrative, and said so

3D renderings

Renderings exist to settle decisions before money is spent on them. They are illustrative rather than photographic predictions — light, colour and material appearance will differ from the built result, and we say so rather than letting a client expect an exact match.

Program 02 · Drawn to scale

Floor plans & space planning

Most layout problems are clearance problems: a door that cannot open fully, a walkway too tight once a chair is pulled out. Drawing to scale with real furniture dimensions catches those on paper, which is the cheapest place to find them.

Material & finish boards
